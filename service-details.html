High-Concurrency Enterprise Architecture

Transforming Complex Business Operations into High-Speed Software

Off-the-shelf software rarely fits intricate business workflows, multi-warehouse supply chains, or fast-growing digital operations. At Classic IT, we build tailor-made enterprise software from the ground up — giving your team complete operational autonomy, unmatched execution speed, and 100% intellectual property ownership.

Our engineers leverage modern component-driven frontends (React, Next.js), decoupled microservices (Node.js, FastAPI, Python), and distributed database architectures (PostgreSQL, Redis). Every production release passes automated OWASP Top-10 security scans, load tests simulating tens of thousands of concurrent sessions, and zero-downtime deployment pipelines.
