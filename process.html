Frequently Asked Questions About Our Process

Everything you need to know about working with our team, sprint schedules, and milestone handovers.

We maintain an agile Change Request (CR) management framework. If you require changes outside the signed SRS, our tech leads review the request, present a clear timeline and cost delta, and only implement it upon your explicit written approval without disrupting ongoing sprints.

You get direct access to your dedicated Project Manager and Lead Architect via a private Slack channel and scheduled bi-weekly Google Meet sprint calls. There is zero telephone-game communication barrier.

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, and assets. Upon milestone completion, code is committed to your own private GitHub/GitLab account and cloud infrastructure. We sign strict Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before discovery begins.

Every software project includes a complimentary 90-day post-launch warranty period during which any defects or bugs matching the SRS are resolved at zero extra cost. Afterward, we offer flexible 24/7 SLA maintenance retainers.
