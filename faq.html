Category 01

General Engagement & Pricing Models

Since 2011, Classic IT has delivered over 500 enterprise platforms across Retail & E-Commerce, Manufacturing, Supply Chain & Courier Logistics, Pharmaceuticals, Educational Institutions, and Healthcare conglomerates. We work with both growing mid-sized companies needing process automation and multinational enterprises requiring high-concurrency cloud architectures.
We offer two transparent engagement models:
  • Fixed-Price Milestone Model: Ideal for projects with a clearly defined Scope of Work (SRS). You receive a guaranteed price cap with milestone-tied release payments. No hidden surprises.
  • Dedicated Agile Team Model: Best for evolving products and ongoing scaling where you require dedicated full-stack engineers, UI/UX designers, and QA leads working as an extension of your company.

Category 02

Agile Development, Timelines & Staging

Standard software implementations (such as our POS, Pharmacy, Courier, or School management platforms) can be configured and deployed in 2 to 5 business days. Custom enterprise ERP systems, microservice backends, and multi-tier mobile applications typically follow a 4 to 12-week roadmap broken down into 2-week agile sprints with bi-weekly live staging demos.
We practice agile flexibility while protecting your project budget. If new features arise that were not covered in the original SRS agreement, our tech leads prepare a transparent Change Request (CR) assessment detailing timeline and cost adjustments. Work only proceeds upon your explicit written authorization without delaying active sprint deliverables.
Yes! You will never wait until the end of a project to see your product. Every 14 days, completed sprint builds are deployed to a private staging URL where your team can test actual working software on live devices. We conduct bi-weekly review calls to gather your feedback directly.

Source Code Ownership & Infrastructure

You retain 100% legal ownership of all intellectual property, source code, database architectures, and design assets. Upon final milestone completion, we transfer the private GitHub / GitLab repository, deployment scripts, and cloud master keys to your organization with zero proprietary vendor lock-in.

Category 04

Security Audits & 24/7 SLA Support

Classic IT operates under ISO 27001 information security standards. Every production release passes automated OWASP Top-10 vulnerability scans, SQL injection prevention, cross-site scripting (XSS) defenses, and end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption. We also configure automated daily cloud backups and Web Application Firewalls (WAF).
Every custom software deployment includes a 90-day complimentary warranty period. During this window, any defects, discrepancies, or software bugs related to the agreed SRS are resolved with the highest priority at zero additional cost to you.
Following the warranty period, we offer flexible 24/7 Enterprise SLA retainers guaranteeing 99.98% cloud uptime, automated database replication, dependency security patches, and a guaranteed 1-hour critical emergency response SLA via our dedicated client hotline.
